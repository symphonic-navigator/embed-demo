# Embedding playground

A small browser page by Second Circuit where curious non-experts play with real vector embeddings
(NVIDIA Nemotron-3-Embed-1B, quantised, running locally in the browser).

- Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-16-embedding-playground-design.md`
- Brief: `docs/superpowers/overnighter/2026-11-16-embedding-playground-brief.md`
- Plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-16-embedding-playground.md`
- Overnighter report: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-16-embedding-playground-overnighter-report.md`
- Open items: `open-items.md`
- Model tooling: `tools/model/` (export, validate, demo-set embeddings)
- Upload guide: `docs/hf-upload.md`
